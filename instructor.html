Your class

——————

  1. Students open Learn → Classroom on their phone and type the code. They may add a first name or a nickname; nothing else is asked.
  2. With the classroom server their snapshot arrives automatically each time they finish a lesson or quiz (and at most every five minutes otherwise). Without it they tap Copy share code every week and send you the code; paste the codes below.
  3. Tap a row for the module breakdown, drills and the grade sheet. Export the roster or the gradebook as CSV when grades are due.

A class lives in this browser (and on the classroom server when one is configured). Export a backup from the Export section before you clear site data or change devices.
Instructor secret (only if your school set one on the server)
Lets you create classes and read rosters without a magic-link account. It is sent only to the classroom server you configured and stored only in this browser. It is school-wide: this one secret unlocks the roster and student removal for every class on your server, not just yours. On a shared or lab computer, forget it when you finish; your own classes keep working through their per-class instructor key.

Paper return is shown for conversation ("why does the top return have the biggest drawdown?") and is never part of the grade. Progress and quiz data come from the student's own device and are evidence of work, not proof; combine them with in-class exercises and the capstone.

Import share codes

A share code is the student's whole snapshot, compressed. Newer snapshots replace older ones for the same student; an older code never overwrites what you already have.

Gradebook

Weights follow the Teaching Guide (§8): quizzes 20, drills 20, journal 20, forward test 10, capstone 25, participation 5. Four components are scored automatically from the snapshot as a starting point; forward test and capstone are yours to enter, and you can override any automatic score per student.

Export & backup

CSV has one row per student and plain column names (progress_pct, quiz_avg_pct, drills_required_passed…). Names that start with "=" are quoted so a nickname cannot run a formula in your spreadsheet.
Restoring merges: existing classes keep their newer snapshots and your manual grades.

Privacy notice

No emails, no accounts

A student joins with the class code alone. The app creates a random id (like s-k3m9p2x7qa) on their device; that id, an optional name they type, and course statistics are the whole record. You cannot look a student up, and neither can we.

Data minimisation (FERPA-minded)

Snapshots contain lesson ids, quiz percentages, drill scorecards, paper-trading statistics, journal counts and the Daily 5 streak. No journal text, no trade list, no IP address, no device details. The server stores exactly these fields and nothing it did not ask for.

Where it lives

Everything on this page is in this browser (bt.instructor). With the classroom server, snapshots also live in your school's own Cloudflare account for up to 400 days and can be deleted by you per student or by your IT staff per class.

Your part

The name field is for your convenience only; a first name or a seat number is enough. Treat exports like any grade sheet: keep them in the LMS or your institution's drive, not in email threads. Removing a student here deletes their server record and tells their device to stop sending.

Leagues

Run a paper-trading competition alongside the class. Create the league on the Leagues page, give students its league code, then paste their share codes here to see the standings. Standings rank simulated returns for discussion: they are not grades, and the top return often carries the biggest drawdown.
