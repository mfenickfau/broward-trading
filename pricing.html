Questions

Is anything locked behind Pro right now?

No. Every page, lesson, quiz and tool is free and works without an account. The code is open source under the MIT licence. If we ever add Pro-only lessons they will be new material, not things you can use today.

Why would I pay, then?

For proof and people. A verified certificate is one an employer, scholarship committee or transfer office can check on this site by id. Instructor tools let a class run a leaderboard and see progress. Priority support means a human answers. The membership also pays the hosting and email bills.

What is the difference between a self-issued and a verified certificate?

Both show the same course and the same requirements (every lesson done, 80% quiz average per module). A self-issued certificate is drawn in your browser from progress saved on your device; anyone could edit that storage, so it is proof for you, not for others. Its id starts with BT-YYYY-L. A verified certificate is issued by our server to a signed-in account and anyone can look it up on the Verify page.

How does sign-in work? Do I need a password?

No passwords. You enter your email and we send a link that signs you in on that device for 30 days. The link expires after 15 minutes and works once. We store your email, whether you are a member, and the names on your certificates; nothing else.

Can I cancel? Can I get a refund?

Planned policy: cancel any time from the billing portal and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Full refund within 14 days of your first payment if you have not issued a verified certificate. Once payments launch, the final policy will be published here before the first charge.

Is Pro connected to any school?

No. Broward Trading is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with Broward College or any institution. Instructors may use the free tools in class; the planned classroom features are opt-in and use nicknames rather than names.

Will Pro give me better signals or make me money?

No. Pro changes nothing about the indicators or the simulator. Signals describe what indicators say right now, not what will happen next. No tier of this site is investment advice, and no real orders are ever placed.

Refunds, billing and disclaimer

Billing (planned). Pro would be a recurring subscription billed monthly or yearly through Stripe. You would see the exact price, currency and renewal date on the checkout page before confirming. Cancelling stops future charges; access continues to the end of the paid period.

Refunds (planned). Full refund within 14 days of the first payment on request, provided no verified certificate has been issued on the account. Renewals are refundable within 7 days if the account was not used in that period. Requests go to the support address shown after sign-in.

Certificates. A certificate, verified or self-issued, recognises completion of a free online course. It is not a licence, qualification, or endorsement of any kind and does not certify competence to trade or advise.

Disclaimer. Broward Trading is a free educational simulator. No real orders are placed and no money changes hands in the simulator. Demo prices are generated, not real; live prices come from the provider you configure and may be delayed. Past performance, including back-test and replay results, does not predict future results. Nothing on this site is investment, tax or legal advice.
