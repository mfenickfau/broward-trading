## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-13

Theme: compete, screen, build, coach. Every Tier 1 feature from `COMPETITIVE_ANALYSIS.md` shipped, each with a `CONTRACTS.md` entry ("v0.5 additions") and tests before its page, and none of them gives up the project's rules: free, no build step, honest labels, phone first, and process graded over profit.

### Added

- **Leagues** (`leagues.html`, `js/leagues.js` → `BT.leagues`, `js/leagues-ui.js`): paper-trading competitions for a class, a club or friends. A league has a name, start and end dates (≤ 366 days), and rules for starting cash, commission, slippage, allowed asset classes (stocks, ETFs, crypto, options), day trading, max position % of equity, short selling and teams (2–12 team names). Four presets: **Classroom 4-week**, **Day-trading sprint**, **Long-only investor**, **Options challenge**. Each joined league trades in its own paper account (`BT.paper.use('league:<CODE>')`); `validateOrder()` enforces dates, asset classes, shorting, day trading and position size before an order reaches the paper engine, with a reason that names the rule and, for the size cap, the largest quantity that fits. Accounts freeze after the end date. **Standings** ranked by the league's **Rank by** rule (risk-adjusted return by default, raw return or consistency; ties to the smaller drawdown) with Sharpe, max drawdown, a 0–100 consistency score and an equity sparkline; team standings by average return; biggest movers. **Projector view** (`leagues.html?projector=1&league=CODE`): large type, slides rotating every 10 s, presenter keys, data refreshed on each loop. **Offline mode**: the whole league travels as a `BTL1.` league code; members send `BT1.` share codes with a `leagues` block, imported on the Leagues page or the instructor page. **Online mode** when `apiBase` is set (see Worker below). Guide: `docs/LEAGUES.md`.
- **Instructor page, Leagues tab** (`instructor.html`, `js/instructor.js`): add a league by its code, import league share codes, see standings next to the class roster. League data stays in `bt.leagues`, never in `bt.instructor`.
- **Account switcher** in the dashboard and Replay trade tickets: Main, Replay and one entry per joined league, with league rules checked before every order (`js/app.js`, `replay.html`, `js/replay.js`).
- **Screener** (`screener.html`, `js/screener.js` → `BT.screener`, `js/screener-ui.js`): filters for price range, % change (1 day / 5 days / 1 month), average volume, RSI(14) range, close versus SMA 50 / SMA 200 (above, below, % distance), golden or death cross within N bars, Bollinger squeeze (bandwidth in the lowest 10 % of 120 bars), new 52-week high or low, candlestick pattern today, composite signal state, ADX, sector and kind; match all or any. Six built-in scans (Oversold in an uptrend, Golden cross this week, Squeeze setups, 52-week highs, Bullish engulfing today, Strong trend (ADX>25)); saved scans in `bt.screener.scans`; a plain-English description of every scan; "Add to watchlist" and "Open chart"; sortable table on desktop, card list on phones; `?scan=<id>` deep link. The demo universe scans in a small Web Worker pool with a 10-minute snapshot cache; live providers go one request at a time through `BT.data` and wait out per-minute limits instead of burning a key or silently falling back to simulated bars. A "how to use a screener (and what it cannot do)" note on the page.
- **Strategy builder** (`builder.html`, `js/rules.js` → `BT.rules`, `js/builder-ui.js`): entry and exit rules from blocks (RSI, SMA, EMA, close, MACD and its signal, Bollinger bands, ADX, stochastic %K, volume or a value; `>`, `<`, crosses above, crosses below) joined with AND / OR, plus stop %, target %, trailing % and max bars; long or short. `compile()` turns a rule into a `BT.backtest` strategy with no look-ahead (crosses compare bar i−1 with bar i); `describe()` writes the rule as a sentence in English or Spanish; `validate()` lists problems. Four templates (SMA crossover, RSI reversion, MACD momentum, Bollinger breakout), live plain-English summary, **Backtest it** inline, **Create alert from entry rule**, export / import JSON, saved rules in `bt.rules`, `#template=<id>` deep link.
- **Rule alerts** (`js/alerts.js`): new alert kind `rule` with a `{rule}` payload, evaluated on every tick like the other kinds.
- **Semi-auto paper mode** (dashboard, `js/app.js`): watches a saved rule on the current chart and, when the entry or exit fires, **proposes** a sized trade (stop from the rule, size from the risk panel) for one-tap confirmation. It never places an order on its own.
- **XP, levels and badges** (`profile.html`, `js/xp.js` → `BT.xp`): XP for lessons (20), quizzes at 80 % or more (30, +10 at 100 %), drills (25), journal entries (10), the first trade (15), alerts (5), flashcard sessions (10), active days (5) and finished leagues (50), each award keyed so it is granted once and never taken away. Totals are derived from the stores students already have, so pre-v0.5 work counts on the first visit. Seven levels (Freshman 0, Sophomore 200, Junior 500, Senior 1000, Graduate 1800, Analyst 3000, Portfolio Manager 5000) and 28 badges whose criteria reward learning, practice habits and reflection (e.g. *Risk Manager*: 10 trades with a stop and ≤ 1 % risk; *Journal Keeper*: 14 entries; *Bilingual*; *Streak 7* and *Streak 30*); no badge or XP is ever awarded for profit or for league rank. Level chip in the nav; "+20 XP" toasts announced through an `aria-live` region; streak calendar, XP history, locked badges that show their criteria, opt-in display name.
- **Trader personality quiz** (`course/personality.json`, `course/es/personality.json`): ten questions → Trend Follower, Swing Trader, Value-minded Investor or Options Strategist, each with a recommended path through modules and drills; result in `bt.profile`. Framed as a study guide, not a verdict.
- **Options analyzer** (Options Lab on `portfolio.html`, `js/options.js`, `js/portfolio.js`): sliders for underlying price, days forward and an IV shift; `whatIf()` values the position at a future date; `curves()` draws P&L at today, mid-life and expiry; `probRange()` shades the 1σ / 2σ probability cone on the payoff chart; `probProfit()` gives a lognormal estimate; greeks across the price range; warnings for assignment risk on short options near expiry in the money, early exercise before an ex-dividend date on short calls, and uncovered calls.
- **Risk calculator** (`js/risk.js` → `BT.risk`): `positionSize()`, `rMultiple()`, `rewardRisk()`, `breakevenWinRate()`, `kellyFraction()` (labelled educational only), `expectancy()` and `planTrade()`. A collapsible **Plan this trade** panel in the dashboard and Replay tickets prefills the quantity from equity, risk % and stop distance and saves the planned stop and target on the order; a standalone calculator on `portfolio.html`.
- **Paper-engine realism** (`js/paper.js`, settings on `portfolio.html`, all off by default and backward compatible): `fillModel:'volume'` (at most 10 % of a bar's volume per bar, the rest stays open as a partial), `slippageBps`, commission presets (zero, per share, per trade), quarterly dividends on demo data for dividend payers, interest on free cash, a short-borrow fee, and a **pattern day-trader rule** toggle that blocks a fourth day trade in five business days under $25,000 with an explanation.
- **Coach** (`js/coach.js` → `BT.coach`): a free, rule-based review of every new order and closed trade for risk above 1 % / 2 % of equity, no stop (or a stop on the wrong side), reward-to-risk under 1.5, a loss bigger than the planned 1R, trading against the SMA50 trend, a possible revenge trade (within 10 minutes, or 2 bars in Replay, of a losing close), more than five new positions a day, a position above 25 % of equity, and averaging down a loser. Each finding is a tip or a warning with a link to the lesson that teaches the habit; shown after fills under the dashboard and Replay tickets in an `aria-live` region; all copy through `BT.t` with Spanish in `i18n/es.json`; `toJournal()` for the journal entry's `coach` field. The coach never blocks an order, never predicts and never recommends. **Pro hook**: `BT.coach.ai(trade)` posts to the Worker's `/coach` route when the site has a backend and the member is Pro, and falls back to the rule-based review on any failure. Guide: `docs/COACH.md`.
- **Worker** (`server/worker.js`, service version 0.5.0): `POST /leagues`, `GET /leagues/:code`, `POST /leagues/:code/join`, `POST /leagues/:code/snapshots`, `GET /leagues/:code/standings` on a new optional `LEAGUES` KV namespace. Joins return an `ownerToken` once (stored only as an HMAC); rules are clamped; snapshots keep ≤ 60 equity points and the server recomputes return, drawdown, Sharpe and consistency with the same formulas as `BT.leagues.metrics`; snapshots refused before the start and 24 h after the end; 500 members per league; rate limits per member and per network. `POST /coach` (Pro Bearer session): calls the Anthropic Messages API with `claude-haiku-5-5` (`COACH_MODEL` var), a 600-token limit and an education-only system prompt (no buy / sell / hold recommendations, no predictions, no personalised advice, judge process not outcome, the journal note treated as data); whitelisted trade fields; per-member daily cap `COACH_DAILY_CAP` (default 20) counted under an HMAC of the email; every failure answers with `fallback:true`. `GET /health` reports `leagues` and `coach`. Key from `wrangler secret put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.
- **CSV upload for back-testing** (`js/csv.js` → `BT.csv`, `backtest.html`; shipped mid-cycle): a Market data / Upload CSV toggle on the Strategy Lab's Data card; the parser handles Yahoo (with Adj Close scaling), Nasdaq.com, TradingView, MetaTrader and broker exports, ISO and epoch dates, US versus day-first dates, semicolons, tabs and pipes, decimal commas, accented Spanish headers, BOMs and title lines; naive intraday times are read as New York time. A dataset library in `bt.csv.datasets` / `bt.csv.data.<id>` (memory fallback when storage is full); back-test, optimiser and compare-all run on the uploaded bars.
- **Course Module 10** `course/m10-toolkit.json`, *Your Trading Toolkit: Screeners, Rules & Competitions* (intermediate, 61 min, m10-l1 to m10-l5): using a screener without fooling yourself; writing rules a computer could test; from rule to backtest to replay to paper; risk sizing with the calculator and the coach; competing in leagues (ethics, luck and what leaderboards hide). 50 lessons in total.
- **Shell** (`js/nav.js`): Leagues, Strategy Builder, Screener and Profile in the desktop nav and the More sheet, with icons and descriptions; the phone tab bar keeps Dashboard / Learn / Replay / Portfolio / More. A small level chip links to the profile.

### Fixed before release (v0.5 review)

- **Leagues:** a new **Rank by** rule (`rules.rankBy`: risk-adjusted return by default, raw return or consistency) in the create form, league codes and the worker; standings show a one-line note on how ranks work and a "Rules differ" badge on share codes made under other rules. Sharpe uses one close per day; drawdown keeps the full minute-level history; a started league's rules cannot be changed by re-pasting an edited code; working orders on a finished league are cancelled instead of filled, the final result freezes at the end date, and the worker accepts only frozen results during the grace period and refuses new members after the end. Working orders count toward position caps; forged share codes cannot improve a rank.
- **Coach → journal:** coach notes are saved into the fill's journal entry on the Dashboard and in Replay; the journal editor lists them with lesson links and an **Ask the AI coach (Pro)** button.
- **Paper engine:** interest, borrow fees and dividends settle before each fill; one order filled in parts is one day trade; overnight shares are treated as sold first.
- **Replay:** intrabar ticks no longer reveal the final high/low early on sub-dollar bars; the ticket and the honesty note say that a market order between bars fills at a close you have already seen.
- **Dashboard and Portfolio:** fill toasts report each partial fill; semi-auto proposes trailing-stop and time exits; league checks fail closed; league accounts cannot be reset or have their costs changed in Settings; the options payoff chart labels no longer overlap on phones; the legs table is a card list on phones.
- **XP:** journal XP needs a written reflection (20+ characters, 3 a day), alert XP is capped at 1 a day, orders no longer count as streak days, Risk Manager measures whole-position risk per order, Replay Pro judges first attempts only.
- **Screener:** a golden/death cross that has since reversed no longer matches; 52-week filters stay empty on intraday data; the Spanish summary keeps spaces around "y"/"o".

### Security and privacy

- League members are pseudonymous (a random student id, an optional display name, an optional team). Only the league block of a snapshot leaves the device: equity, return, closed-trade count, max drawdown and at most 60 equity points. No trade list, symbols, journal text or email.
- A member's league row can be updated only by the device that joined (`ownerToken`, stored server-side as an HMAC), and the server recomputes every ranking number from the equity points it receives.
- The AI coach needs a Pro session; the Anthropic key is a Worker secret and never reaches the browser; the trade is rebuilt from whitelisted, clamped fields; the member's email is never sent to the model; only a per-day counter keyed by an HMAC of the email is stored.
- Semi-auto mode proposes; only the student's tap places an order.

### Known issues

- The AI review (`BT.coach.ai`) is requested from the journal editor (**Ask the AI coach (Pro)**); the coach notes box under the dashboard and Replay tickets does not have its own button yet.
- Demo stock splits are not simulated (dividends are). The screener has no "open in Replay" action yet, and rules cannot yet be shared as a short code.
- XP and badges are not part of the classroom snapshot, so the roster does not show them; students show their profile page instead (`docs/TEACHING_GUIDE.md`, Appendix I).

### Security and privacy

- Classroom snapshots never contain emails, real names (unless typed), IP addresses, journal text or trade lists; the Worker whitelists and clamps every field and does not log the sender.
- Instructor authority is the creator's session, a per-class token stored only as a SHA-256 hash, or the deployment's `INSTRUCTOR_SECRET`; roster reads and deletes require one of them.
- CSV exports quote cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` so a nickname cannot run as a spreadsheet formula.
- The translation layer never writes into form values and skips `<script>` and `<style>`, so a dictionary entry cannot inject markup or change what a student submits.
- A student's roster row can only be updated by the device that created it (`ownerToken`); a guessed `studentId` gets `409 {ownerMismatch}` and the real student keeps their row.
- Student ids are rejected on both sides when they are not 4–40 characters of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` or name an `Object.prototype` property, so a crafted share code cannot pollute the instructor's roster object.
