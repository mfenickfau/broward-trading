              How to read a backtest of your rule

              • No peeking. Each decision uses only the bars up to that point. "Crosses above" compares the previous bar with the current one, and every order fills at the next bar's open, the way it would in real life.
              • Past is not future. A rule that worked on one stretch of history can lose on the next. Test it on several symbols and periods before you trust it.
              • Beware of tuning. If you keep adjusting numbers until the result looks great, you are fitting noise. Prefer simple rules whose results hold up when the numbers move a little.
              • Costs matter. Commissions, slippage and taxes turn many small winners into losers. Leave some slippage in the costs box.
              • Demo prices are simulated. In demo mode each symbol follows a made-up random walk, so results are for practice only. Add a live data key in Dashboard settings to test on real daily bars.
