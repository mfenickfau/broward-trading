About the project

Learn to trade with real tools and zero real risk.

Broward Trading is a free, open educational simulator: a professional-style charting dashboard, a $100,000 paper-trading account, an options lab, a strategy backtester and a six-module course. It was built for students at Broward College and for anyone who wants to understand how markets work before putting a single dollar at risk.

Mission

Why this exists

Most people meet the stock market for the first time through an app that is designed to get them trading quickly, with real money, before they understand what a limit order is. We think the order should be reversed: learn first, practise with pretend money second, and only then decide whether real trading is for you.

Broward Trading is our attempt at that first two steps. It is a classroom-friendly simulator that runs entirely in your browser, costs nothing, asks for no account or personal data, and is honest about its limits. It is being developed alongside students at Broward College in South Florida, but anyone, anywhere, can use it.

Three principles guide every feature we add:

1Education before execution

Every indicator on the dashboard has a plain-English explanation next to it. Every signal lists its reasons. Nothing is a black box.

2Honest about uncertainty

Signals describe what indicators say right now, never what will happen next. Backtests show drawdowns as prominently as returns.

3Free and private

No sign-up, no tracking, no real money. Your portfolio and progress live in your own browser. The code is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript you can read.

How it works

Five pages, one workflow

The site is a set of linked tools that mirror what a disciplined trader actually does: study, plan, practise, review.

Dashboard index.html

Load a ticker, pick a timeframe, toggle overlays and watch a composite signal score the chart from −8 (strong sell) to +8 (strong buy). Every point in the score comes with a reason and a "why". Buy and sell shares into your paper account without leaving the chart.

Learn learn.html

The course: six modules from "what is a stock?" through candlesticks, indicators, risk management and options, to building and backtesting a strategy. Lessons embed live charts, exercises that send you to the dashboard, and quizzes that track your progress.

Portfolio portfolio.html

Your $100,000 paper account. Open positions, pending limit and stop orders, trade history, an equity curve and statistics such as win rate, profit factor, maximum drawdown and Sharpe ratio. Includes an options chain and payoff diagrams for nine classic strategies.

Backtest backtest.html

Pick a rule set (SMA cross, RSI reversion, MACD, Bollinger bounce, breakout, the composite signal or buy-and-hold), tune its parameters, and run it over the chart history. Compare against buy-and-hold and see exactly where it would have entered and exited.

Everything runs in your browserThere is no server. Indicators are computed locally in JavaScript, bars are cached in localStorage, and your paper trades never leave your device. Clearing your browser data resets the simulator; use the export buttons if you want to keep a record.
Data sources & accuracy

Demo mode versus live mode

Out of the box, the simulator runs in demo mode: prices are generated by a seeded random walk, so the chart for a given ticker always looks the same, moves realistically, and ticks every few seconds, but it is not the real price of that company. Demo mode is perfect for learning how indicators and orders behave, and it is clearly labelled with an amber demo pill on every page.

If you want real market data, you can switch to live mode by pasting a free API key on the dashboard. We deliberately support only providers with a genuinely free tier so that nobody has to pay to learn.

Limits are those published by each provider at the time of writing and may change. The app enforces a per-provider rate limiter and caches bars (60 seconds intraday, 10 minutes daily) so you stay inside the free allowance. When a key is missing, a limit is hit or a request fails, the app falls back to demo data and tells you why.

Expect a 15-minute delayFree market-data plans in the United States almost always deliver quotes delayed by about 15 minutes, because exchanges charge for real-time feeds. For learning this does not matter at all; for real trading it would. Treat any "live" price here as recent, not current.
How accurate are the indicators?The indicator maths (SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR, ADX, stochastic, VWAP, Ichimoku and more) follows the standard textbook definitions and is covered by automated tests in the tests/ folder. Small differences from other platforms usually come from how many bars of history are loaded, since EMA-based indicators need a long warm-up.

What the paper broker does and does not simulate

  • Does: market, limit, stop and stop-limit orders filled against each bar's high and low; short selling; optional commissions and slippage in the backtester; options priced with the Black-Scholes model and a synthetic chain with bid/ask spreads and a volatility smile.
  • Does not: real order books, partial fills, borrow fees, dividends, margin calls, trading halts, or the real options market. Real options quotes require paid data; ours are model prices, which is why the options lab is labelled "synthetic".

These simplifications make the simulator kinder than a real account. If a strategy barely works here, it will not work with real friction. That is a feature: the point is to learn the mechanics and the discipline, not to find a money machine.

Frequently asked

Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored in your browser's local storage. The downside is that clearing site data, or switching devices, resets your paper account and course progress. Export options and cloud sync are on the roadmap.

Is the BUY / SELL signal good?

It is a transparent, rule-based score built from standard indicators. It is useful for learning how those indicators agree or disagree. It is not a profitable trading system and we never claim it is. Backtest it yourself on the Backtest page to see.

Why are demo prices for AAPL not Apple's real price?

Demo mode generates a realistic-looking series from the ticker's name so that lessons are repeatable and the site works with no API key. Add a free key on the dashboard to see real (delayed) prices.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. Every page is responsive; the chart panes shrink and the control rail stacks below them. Drawing trend lines is easier with a mouse or a stylus.

Can I run it myself or contribute?

Yes. The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step. Open index.html in a browser or serve the folder with any static file server. Design tokens and components are documented in docs/BRAND.md and module interfaces in CONTRACTS.md.

Is my API key safe?

Your key is stored only in your own browser and sent only to the provider you chose, directly from your device. We never see it. Use a free-tier key and revoke it from the provider's dashboard if you ever share your computer.
