    What a result means

    Verified

    The id exists on the Broward Trading server. It was issued to a signed-in account after the server received the course record: every lesson marked done and a quiz average of at least 80% in each module. The name shown is the one the holder entered; we do not verify legal names and we never show the holder's email.

    Self-issued (unverified)

    Ids whose third group starts with L (for example BT-2026-LK7Q2M), and the older eight-character ids such as BT-1A2B3C4D, were created inside a student's browser from progress saved on their device. The site cannot confirm them because nothing was sent to a server. They are still a meaningful personal record, but treat them like a self-reported score. Students who want a checkable certificate can sign in on the Pro page once accounts are available.

    Not found

    No certificate with that id exists on the server. Check for a mistyped character first (the font on a printout can make 8 and B look alike). If the id still cannot be found, the certificate did not come from this site.

    What a certificate is not

    Completion of a free online course. It is not a licence, a professional qualification, or evidence of trading skill, and Broward Trading is not affiliated with any college or institution. Nothing on this site is investment advice.
