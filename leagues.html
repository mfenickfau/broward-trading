        How the standings are calculated
        • Return: equity now versus the league's starting cash. Ties go to the smaller drawdown.
        • Max drawdown: the biggest fall from a peak to a later low. Smaller means the account took less pain.
        • Sharpe ratio: average return divided by how much it swung, annualised. It needs at least three equity points and rewards steady gains over wild swings.
        • Consistency (0 to 100): 60 % from how often the account rose between equity points, 40 % from keeping drawdown small (0 % scores full marks, 20 % or more scores none).
        • Equity points are recorded when you trade or open the dashboard on the league account; at most 60 points are shared.
